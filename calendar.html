How a race weekend runs

  1. Saturday: round practiceOpen practice for everyone entered, on the layout you will race the next day.
  2. Sunday: race daySigning-on, compulsory practice, heats, then B-final and final by class.
  3. Mid-month: practice dayA standalone practice day roughly two weeks after each round, including pre-ARKS sessions for drivers without a licence.

Subscribing keeps these dates up to date on your phone, cancellations included. A downloaded file is a snapshot and will never change again.

One exception. Round 7 practice is on Friday 4 September, not the Saturday, because the Southern Championships run on the Saturday.
