The track

A Motorsport UK licensed circuit on MOD land, with fast straights and technical corner complexes, split into three timed sectors.

Barton Stacey,
just off the A303.

From the east (Andover and Thruxton side): the Barton Stacey exit is just after the BP petrol station. Turn left towards Longparish, then immediately right at the Raymond Browns sign.

From the west (Bullington Cross side): take the Barton Stacey exit, then turn right towards Longparish over the A303 bridge. Turn right at the Raymond Browns sign.

Either way, the circuit is a quarter of a mile down that lane.

Site rules

No tobacco products

Smoking and vaping are not permitted anywhere on site.

No dogs

Dogs are banned by order of the Ministry of Defence, who own the land. There are no exceptions and the club cannot make any.
