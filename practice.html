Practice days

No entry form, no championship pressure, and sessions for drivers who do not hold a licence yet. Just turn up, sign on, and get your wristband.

There is no form to fill in.

Forest Edge does not take practice day bookings online. Come to race control before you go out, pay for the day, and collect your wristband. That is the whole process.

Sessions are grouped by class and run in rotation through the day, so you get repeated runs rather than one long stint.

Pre-ARKS sessions need no licence.

If you have never raced, the pre-ARKS sessions are the way in. They run for Bambino and Cadet drivers and for Junior and Senior drivers, and you do not need a Motorsport UK licence to take part.

When you are ready to race for points, the ARKS test is the next step. The club can arrange yours. Email arks@fekc.co.uk to book.
