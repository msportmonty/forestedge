Terms of use

The terms on which Forest Edge Kart Club publishes this website. They cover the site itself, not entry to a race meeting.

Last updated 23 August 2026

Who runs this site

This website is published by Forest Edge Kart Club, Barton Stacey, Winchester SO21 3BF. Contact the Competition Secretary at compsec@fekc.co.uk or 07555 112012. By using the site you accept these terms.

What the site is for

It tells you when the club races, what it costs, where the circuit is and who to ask. You are welcome to read it, print it, link to any page, and share it.

Please do not use the site to break the law, to try to gain access to anything not published on it, to scrape it in a way that degrades it for other people, or to republish it as though it were yours.

Where the site is not the last word

Race meetings are governed by the Motorsport UK regulations, the club’s Championship and Supplementary Regulations, and the instructions of the Clerk of the Course on the day. Where anything here disagrees with those, those win. This is a website, not a rulebook.

Dates, fees, timings and class lists are published in good faith and kept as current as the club can. They do change, sometimes at short notice and sometimes for weather. Check with race control or the Competition Secretary before travelling a long way or spending money on the strength of a page here.

Entries, membership and timing

Entering a race, joining the club, live timing and results all happen on Alpha Timing’s website. Those are their services under their terms, not the club’s. The club will never ask you to pay entry or membership fees by bank transfer.

Links to other sites

Links to Alpha Timing, Motorsport UK, YouTube, Google Maps, the NSPCC and anywhere else are there because they are useful. The club does not control those sites and is not responsible for their content.

Photographs, words and branding

The photographs on this site were taken by photographers who retain their own copyright and have allowed the club to use them. Please do not copy them for your own use without asking. The Forest Edge Kart Club name and logo belong to the club.

If you would like to use a photograph, email compsec@fekc.co.uk and the club will put you in touch with the photographer.

The data files

The JSON files on the data page are published deliberately so other people can reuse them. You may use them for any purpose, commercial or not, provided you credit Forest Edge Kart Club with a link back, you do not present the data as your own, and you do not sell the data itself as a product.

They are provided as they are. The club does not warrant that they are complete or error free, and does not commit to keeping any particular field or file in place.

Racing is dangerous

Motorsport is dangerous. Nothing on this website is instruction, coaching or safety advice, and reading it is not preparation for going on track. Competing, marshalling or spectating at Forest Edge is subject to Motorsport UK regulations, the signing-on conditions on the day, and the notices displayed at the circuit.

The club takes care over what it publishes, but the site is provided as it is and without warranties. To the extent the law allows, the club is not liable for loss arising from relying on the site, from it being unavailable, or from anything on a site it links to.

Nothing in these terms limits or excludes the club’s liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, for fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation, or for anything else that cannot lawfully be limited or excluded. If you are using the site as a consumer, these terms do not affect your statutory rights.

Privacy and accessibility

How the club handles information is set out in the privacy policy. How the site is built to be usable, and where it currently falls short, is in the accessibility statement.

Changes, and which law applies

These terms may change. Any change is published on this page with a new date at the top, and applies from the moment it appears.

These terms are governed by the law of England and Wales, and the courts of England and Wales have jurisdiction.
